Install on an Intel Mac

macOS 12 or newer, Intel processor (for example a 2019 MacBook). This build does not run on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3). About 3 GB are downloaded during installation and about 8 GB of free disk space are needed. To check your Mac: Apple menu → About This Mac → Processor must say “Intel”.

  1. On the download page click Download for Intel Mac. Open the downloaded WhisperMedical-Installer-IntelMac.zip (Safari opens it by itself) to get WhisperMedical Installer.
  2. Right-click WhisperMedical Installer and choose Open, then Open again. (The installer is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once. Please do not turn Gatekeeper off for your whole Mac.)
  3. Click Install. The installer downloads the program, checks every file automatically, copies Medical Lecture Transcriber to Applications and finishes.
  4. Open Medical Lecture Transcriber from Applications. Whisper Large-v3 is already inside — nothing is downloaded and no internet is needed to use it.

If your connection drops, open the installer again: it continues where it stopped.

Uninstall

Drag Medical Lecture Transcriber from Applications to the Trash.
